AstroLift Gym Management

A full-stack platform that replaces disconnected membership, payment, attendance, and workout records with one role-based operating system for gym administrators, trainers, and members.

RequirementsProcess MappingRBACUser StoriesKPIsTech Stack

01Business Problem & Objectives

Gym teams commonly track members, renewals, attendance, payments, and workout plans in separate spreadsheets or messages. Staff repeat data entry, overdue memberships are hard to spot, trainers lack a shared view of member plans, and management cannot see reliable operational performance.

  • Objective 1: Create one source of truth for memberships, payments, workouts, and attendance.
  • Objective 2: Match system access to admin, trainer, and member responsibilities.
  • Objective 3: Reduce repetitive administration and make expiring or overdue memberships visible.
  • Objective 4: Give management timely KPIs for retention, revenue, and facility usage.

02As-Is vs To-Be Process

As-Is: Staff registers a member on paper or a spreadsheet → payment is recorded separately → attendance is marked manually → trainers share plans through messages → management combines records later for reporting.

To-Be: Admin creates a member profile and activates a plan → payment status updates the membership → attendance is recorded against the same account → trainer assigns and reviews workouts → role-based dashboards show current actions and KPIs.

03Functional Requirements

  • FR1: The system authenticates users and displays features according to admin, trainer, or member role.
  • FR2: Admins can create member records, assign plans, and manage activation, expiry, renewal, or suspension.
  • FR3: Staff can record payments and view outstanding or completed payment status.
  • FR4: Trainers can create and assign workout programmes and review member progress.
  • FR5: Staff can record attendance and retrieve attendance history by member and date.
  • FR6: Dashboards summarise active memberships, payment status, attendance, and workout activity.

05User Story & Acceptance Criteria

As a gym administrator, I want to renew a member's plan and record payment in one workflow, so that access and financial records remain consistent.

  • Given an existing member and a valid plan, when payment is confirmed, then the new membership dates and paid status are saved together.
  • Given payment fails or is cancelled, when the workflow ends, then the current membership remains unchanged.
  • Given a successful renewal, when the admin returns to the dashboard, then the member no longer appears in the expiring list.
