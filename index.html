01 / About

The bridge between business & technology

I'm a final-year BSc (Hons) Software Engineering undergraduate at NSBM Green University (University of Plymouth, UK), moving into business analysis. My engineering background means I speak the language of developers — and I love the other half of the job just as much: sitting with stakeholders, asking the right questions, and shaping messy needs into clear, testable requirements.

Across my projects I've gathered requirements, mapped processes, defined role-based access, and designed analytics dashboards that turn raw sales and attendance data into decisions. I care about clarity, structure, and building things people actually use.

Right now I'm looking for a Business Analyst internship where I can learn from a strong team and contribute from day one.
